Projectile Motion VR Lab

HSC Physics, Module 5: Advanced Mechanics

On Quest 3: open this page in the Meta Quest Browser and tap Enter VR. The console floats in front of you; point a controller (or pinch with your hand) at a button to press it. If the button says VR isn’t available, open the page in its own browser tab rather than inside another page.

On a computer: drag the scene to look around, scroll to zoom, click the console, and press Space to launch.

Try this: turn Answers off, calculate the range and time of flight on paper, then launch to check. Compare 30° and 60° at the same speed, then add launch height and see what changes.
