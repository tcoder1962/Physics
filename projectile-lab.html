How to play

A projectile only feels gravity once it leaves the cannon. Its horizontal speed never changes, while its vertical speed drops by g every second. Every mode is built around seeing that.

Modes

  • Sandbox: change angle, speed, height and planet. Each shot leaves a coloured trail so you can compare. Try 30° and 60° at the same speed.
  • Hit the target: land inside the ring. First-shot hits score 100, then 70, 50, 30, 20.
  • Predict the range: the problem is set for you. Work out the range, enter it, and fire to check. Within 2% scores 100.
  • Falling target: a crate hangs high above the range and drops the moment you fire. Hit it in mid-air before it reaches the ground. Think about where to aim when the ball and the crate are both falling.

Dragging the 3D view in any other view switches to Free.
